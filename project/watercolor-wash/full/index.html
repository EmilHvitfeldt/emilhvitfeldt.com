Path

Curve tool: click a stroke, then drag its anchors (squares) and handles (circles) to change its trajectory. Handles keep the curve smooth; hold Alt to move one on its own. Double-click the path to add an anchor, double-click an anchor to remove it. Timing and pressure stay as recorded.

Timing and pressure

Paint

Applies to the whole painting.

End hold is how many seconds the video keeps running after the last stroke, so it can finish drying. Lower max pigment keeps slow strokes saturated instead of dark. Dry fade is how much color is lost while drying.

Whole-animation speed

Rewrites the timing of every stroke and cue. 2 = twice as fast (half as long), 1.5 = 50% faster, 0.5 = twice as slow. Undo reverts it.

Tracing image

Or drop or paste an image onto the canvas.

With the Image tool: drag to move, scroll to zoom.
